AI & Business Automation

AI Lead Assistant for Service Businesses

AI-powered website assistant that helps service businesses engage visitors, answer questions from their own documents, qualify high-intent leads, and capture enquiries — with instant email alerts on every new lead and an admin dashboard to manage conversations, leads, and the knowledge base.

Conversational AI: Context-aware assistant with quick replies and Gemini-powered responses, tuned to each business's tone and services.
RAG Retrieval: Answers grounded in the business's own FAQs and documents via Postgres vector search (pgvector + Gemini embeddings), not prompt-stuffing.
Lead Capture & Database: Detects high-intent messages, surfaces an enquiry form, and stores structured leads, conversations, and messages in Supabase.
Email Notifications: New leads are sent to the business instantly via Resend, as a best-effort step that never blocks lead capture.
Admin Dashboard: Password-protected panel for leads with status tracking, full conversation history, knowledge-base management, and branding & prompt settings.
JavaScript Serverless Functions Gemini API Supabase / Postgres pgvector (RAG) Resend Vercel
