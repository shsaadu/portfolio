FLAGSHIP PROJECT
AI & Business Automation

Assist Street — AI assistants for businesses

A platform that puts an AI assistant on any business's website. It answers customers from the business's own documents, in the customer's own language, spots people ready to act, and emails the lead to the owner, with a dashboard to manage it all.

One-line embed: a single script tag adds the widget to any site (Shadow DOM, so the host site's styles can't break it), restricted to the websites each business allows.
RAG retrieval: answers grounded in each business's own FAQs and documents via Postgres vector search (pgvector + Gemini embeddings), not prompt-stuffing.
Multilingual: replies in the visitor's language, with 12 interface languages including right-to-left Arabic.
Intent & hand-off: every reply also reports structured intent, language, and whether a person needs to step in.
Lead capture & admin: leads saved to Supabase and emailed via Resend; per-business admin accounts, conversation history, knowledge-base management, and rate limiting.
JavaScript Serverless Functions Gemini API Supabase / Postgres pgvector (RAG) Resend Vercel
